A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Many interests.
One through-line.

I'm Nicole Tran, a creative professional working across photography, modeling, short-form video, social media strategy and public-interest storytelling. I move between being behind the camera, in front of it and shaping the message around it.

My work brings together visual instincts and audience-first communication: professional photography, video editing, Adobe Creative Cloud, platform-native social content and analytics that help identify what is resonating.

I'm especially interested in work that makes people feel something, understand something or look at something a little differently. That can mean documenting the energy of a live event, creating brand content or translating a public policy issue into something approachable.

I value curiosity, frankness, humor and work that feels human rather than overly polished.
